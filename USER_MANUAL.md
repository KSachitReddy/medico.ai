## What is medico.ai?

**medico.ai** is a free tool that helps you find cheaper alternatives to expensive branded medicines — with the same active ingredient and the same effect.

When your doctor prescribes "Crocin", medico.ai shows you that the exact same drug (Paracetamol 500mg) is available at a Jan Aushadhi store for **90% less**.

> ⚕️ **Important:** medico.ai provides information only. Always consult your doctor or pharmacist before switching from one medicine to another.

## How to Search for a Medicine

### Method 1 — Type the Medicine Name

This is the simplest way. You only need to know the name on the medicine box or your prescription.

**Step 1:** On the home screen, tap the search bar in the centre of the page

**Step 2:** Type the name of your medicine
- You can type the brand name (e.g. *Crocin*, *Dolo*, *Combiflam*)
- Or the generic name (e.g. *Paracetamol*, *Ibuprofen*)
- Don't worry about spelling — the app handles common spelling mistakes

**Step 3:** Results appear automatically as you type — tap any result to see full details

---

### Method 2 — Upload a Prescription (AI Reader)

If you have a doctor's prescription and don't want to type each medicine one by one, you can upload a photo and let medico.ai read it for you.

**Step 1:** On the home screen, tap **"Upload Prescription"**

**Step 2:** Take a photo of your prescription or choose one from your gallery
- Make sure the photo is clear and well-lit
- All text should be visible and not blurry

**Step 3:** Tap **"Scan Prescription"**

**Step 4:** medico.ai reads the prescription and automatically searches for every medicine on it

**Step 5:** You see a full list of all medicines with their cheaper alternatives in one screen

> 💡 **Tip:** For best results, photograph the prescription on a flat white surface in good lighting.

## How to Upload a Prescription

### Tips for a Good Scan

| Do This ✅ | Avoid This ❌ |
|---|---|
| Use natural daylight or bright light | Dark or dim lighting |
| Place prescription flat on a table | Holding it in your hand (causes blur) |
| Make sure all text is in the frame | Cutting off part of the prescription |
| Keep the camera still | Moving while taking the photo |
| Use the original prescription | A photocopy of a photocopy |

### What Happens After Upload

1. medico.ai sends the image to an AI model (Claude by Anthropic)
2. The AI reads and extracts all medicine names
3. Each medicine is automatically searched in the database
4. You see results for all medicines on one screen
5. You can tap any medicine to see its full alternatives and savings

### What If the AI Misses a Medicine?

If a medicine from your prescription doesn't appear in the results:
- Scroll down to check if it was listed under a different name
- Use the search bar to manually type that medicine name
- The AI reads most handwriting but may occasionally miss unclear text

## Finding a Jan Aushadhi Store Near You

Jan Aushadhi stores are government-run pharmacies that sell generic medicines at the lowest prices. There are over **10,000 stores across India**.

### How to Find the Nearest Store

**Step 1:** On any medicine result page, tap **"Find Nearby Jan Aushadhi Store"**

**Step 2:** Allow location access when your browser asks

**Step 3:** A map appears showing Jan Aushadhi stores near you with:
- Store name and address
- Distance from your location
- Opening hours (where available)

**Step 4:** Tap any store pin for directions

> 💡 **Tip:** You can also find Jan Aushadhi stores by calling the helpline: **1800-180-8080** (toll free)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are generic medicines as effective as branded ones?**
Yes. Generic medicines contain the exact same active ingredient at the same dosage. They are required by law to meet the same safety and efficacy standards as branded medicines. The only difference is the name and the price.

---

**Q: Is medico.ai free to use?**
Yes. medico.ai is completely free. There are no subscriptions, no ads, and no hidden charges. It is a civic tool built for public benefit.

---

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
No. medico.ai works without any login or account. Just open the app and start searching.

---

**Q: Can I trust the prices shown?**
Prices are sourced from the official PM Jan Aushadhi Pariyojana drug list and verified pharmacy platforms. Branded medicine prices may vary slightly by location and pharmacy. Always confirm the final price at the pharmacy counter.

---

**Q: What if my medicine is not in the database?**
medico.ai currently covers the most commonly prescribed medicines in India. If your medicine is not found, try searching by the active ingredient name (ask your doctor or pharmacist for this). We are continuously adding more medicines.

---

**Q: Is it safe to switch to a generic medicine?**
Generics are medically equivalent to branded medicines. However, always consult your doctor or pharmacist before switching, especially for chronic conditions like diabetes, blood pressure, or thyroid.

---

**Q: My prescription is in Telugu / Hindi — will the AI still read it?**
The AI prescription reader works best with English text. If your prescription is in a regional language, use the manual search bar to type the medicine name.

---

**Q: What is Jan Aushadhi?**
The Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) is a Government of India scheme that provides quality generic medicines at affordable prices through dedicated Jan Aushadhi stores across the country. It is funded by the government and available to every citizen.

---
